FAMILY A — INDUSTRIAL SCHEMATIC / HMI  (extends the tech-tree direction)
=============================================================================

--- A / MENU ---
Main menu for a near-future corporate 4X strategy game, titled "PROJECT IO".
Industrial-schematic / HMI aesthetic: etched groove lines, thin corner-bracket
frames, terraced flat bevels (highlight + shadow edge-lines, no gradient),
faint scanline and wear texture. Near-black background #0F0F14 with a faint
cool tint. Flat vector, absolutely no gradients or glows. One accent pair only:
amber/gold (#E8A33D) for primary, saturated cyan (#3FC9E8) for secondary.
Monospaced technical labels. Centred column: seed field with "Roll" button,
"Resources: Sparse / Lean / Standard" radio row, "New Game" and "Quit" buttons
as flat outlined rectangles. Restrained, cold, precise. 1280x720.

--- A / SHELL ---
In-game screen of a near-future corporate 4X, industrial-schematic / HMI style.
Near-black #0F0F14. Flat vector, no gradients. Amber + cyan accents only.
Layout: 56px left icon rail of 13 square glyph slots; top-left hexagonal corp
emblem + name; top-centre "BALANCE / STOCKPILE / NET" data strip; top-right date
and speed buttons I-V; bottom-left comms dock; bottom-centre selection panel
listing tile buildings in a table; bottom-right circular minimap with a row of 6
lens glyphs. Central map is a flat top-down hex grid, terrain by colour, small
etched building glyphs. Everything reads like an engineering console. 1280x720.

FAMILY B — NUCLEAR-ERA CONTROL ROOM / ATOMPUNK
=============================================================================

--- B / MENU ---
Main menu for a near-future corporate 4X titled "PROJECT IO". 1960s
nuclear-control-room / atompunk aesthetic: heavy enamelled metal panels, visible
rivets and seams, chunky toggle switches, phosphor-green readout digits, radium
dial faces, yellow-black hazard chevrons at panel edges. Warm dark charcoal
background, not pure black. Accent palette: hazard amber (#F0B429) and radium
green (#7DE07D), used sparingly. Tactile, mechanical, slightly worn. Centred
control cluster: seed readout with a physical "ROLL" key, a three-position
rotary "RESOURCES" selector, illuminated "NEW GAME" and "QUIT" push-buttons.
Flat shading, screen-print texture, no photoreal reflections. 1280x720.

--- B / SHELL ---
In-game screen of a near-future corporate 4X, 1960s nuclear-control-room style.
Warm dark charcoal panels, enamelled metal, rivets, hazard chevrons, phosphor
readouts. Accents: hazard amber and radium green only. Layout: 56px left rail of
13 backlit square switch-glyphs; top-left round corp badge + name on a metal
nameplate; top-centre "BALANCE / STOCKPILE / NET" as segmented phosphor gauges;
top-right date and speed keys I-V; bottom-left comms teletype dock; bottom-centre
selection panel as a labelled instrument readout of tile buildings; bottom-right
circular radar-style minimap with 6 lens switches. Central map is a flat top-down
hex grid glowing faintly like a plotting table. 1280x720.

FAMILY C — DEEP-SPACE MINIMALISM / OBSERVATORY
=============================================================================

--- C / MENU ---
Main menu for a near-future corporate 4X titled "PROJECT IO". Deep-space
minimalist / observatory aesthetic: near-black #0F0F14, vast negative space,
hairline 1px strokes, faint star-field and thin orbital-arc motifs. One warm

EXTRA — PLANETARY TILE RENDERING + CAMERA
=============================================================================
Three-panel comparison of a strategy-game planet surface. Panel 1 "zoomed out":
straight top-down view, semi-realistic terrain (mountains, rivers, forest, desert,
coast) like Civilization VI, no visible hex grid. Panel 2 "zoomed in, tile
selected": camera dropped to a low oblique angle (~35 degrees from horizontal) so
mountains and buildings have readable height and cast flat shadows; a single hex
tile outlined in white, its 6 edges visible only on that tile and its neighbours.
Panel 3 "close, building focus": same oblique angle, small stylised industrial
buildings and a settlement skyline on the tiles, hex edges shown only under the
cursor. Near-black UI chrome #0F0F14, flat vector overlays, amber selection
outline. Cohesive art style across all three.

EXTRA — ICON / GLYPH SHEET  (restyle of existing vector glyphs, not new assets)
=============================================================================
A vector icon sheet for a near-future corporate 4X strategy game, on a near-black
#0F0F14 background. ~24 small monochrome glyphs in a grid, each in a 2r box,
single accent colour amber #E8A33D, flat vector, no gradients, consistent 2px
stroke weight. Glyphs: extraction site (ore crystal), processing facility (square),
port (triangle), logistics hub (hexagon with centre dot), military base (shield),
market centre (circle with cross), settlement (tiered skyline), ruin (broken
skyline), convoy (chevron), unit (figure), HQ (ringed star), survey (magnifier),

population, continent). Uniform silhouette weight, readable at 16px.
