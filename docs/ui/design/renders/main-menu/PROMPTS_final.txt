ROUND 3 VERDICTS (Joe)
- CV5 (bone white + one hot accent) is the clear favourite. BUT its ember-red
  accent contrasts the current house thinking (amber + blue). For the final
  render: keep the whole CV5 palette EXCEPT swap the red for gold/orange.
- Dark background #0F0F14 is essential and non-negotiable.
- The round-3 layout reads like "an item showcase", not a menu — too much empty
  space with one clean object in the bottom-right. The final render needs a
  background element to fill the negative space and make it feel finished.
- Fill it with a synthesis of two liked things, both drawn as the backdrop:
    * mainC's "big circles and lines" (concentric orbital rings, leader lines)
    * mainC1 / M1's "nice world" (the line-art planet)
  Not yet decided which is stronger alone — so this render COMBINES them and we
  judge the combination.

=============================================================================
FINAL RENDER — full prompt
=============================================================================
Main menu for a near-future corporate 4X strategy game titled "PROJECT IO",
subtitle "Near-future corporate 4X". Deep-space minimalist / observatory
aesthetic, flat vector, hairline 1px strokes, thin geometric sans-serif type,
no gradients, no texture.

PALETTE (CV5, red swapped for gold):
  - Background: near-black #0F0F14 with a faint cool tint. Essential — no
    lighter background.
  - Primary text: bone white #ECE7DD.
  - Secondary / muted text: dim grey.
  - Single accent: warm gold / amber #E8A33D. Used ONLY on the "New Game"
    button and the active "Resources" segment, and as the lit tile in the hex
    cluster. Everything else is bone white or hairline grey.

BACKGROUND HERO (fills the negative space — this is what makes it a menu, not a
showcase): a large line-art planet, off-centre toward the upper right, wrapped
by 2-3 concentric hairline orbital rings / arcs with a few small body dots on
them, tick marks on the rings, and thin leader lines connecting a dot or two to
tiny labels. Drawn at ~30-40% opacity in bone-white hairlines so it sits BEHIND
the controls and bleeds across the whole frame, tying the left and right halves
of the composition together. Quiet, precise, instrument-like — not busy.

CONTROLS (left-centre, calm and unmistakable, over the backdrop):
  - small-caps "PROJECT IO" over its subtitle
  - a thin-ruled seed field with "Roll" and "Copy seed" as text buttons
  - a "Sparse / Lean / Standard" segmented control, active segment in gold
  - a dim "Bodies: 5 (fixed)" readout
  - "New Game" as a gold ghost/outlined button (the one warm element), "Quit"
    as a bone-white ghost button

FOREGROUND CORNER: in the bottom-right, a small cluster of ~12-20 hexagonal map
tiles in OBLIQUE projection — camera low, ~30-40 degrees above horizontal, the
close-up game angle, NOT top-down. Slight extruded tile height so side faces
show; a couple carry low relief (a ridge, a small building block) with
hard-edged flat shadows. Terrain by muted desaturated grey fill; hex edges
visible; exactly ONE tile lit in gold #E8A33D. The cluster sits low, partly
cropped by the frame edge, ~70-80% opacity.

1280x720. Layout ref: ~/io_shell_captures_20260830/main_menu.png
Style ref: ../techtree/refs/ON-STYLE/mono, ../techtree/refs/ON-STYLE/hexclean
