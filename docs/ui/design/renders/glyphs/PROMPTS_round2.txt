PROJECT IO — GLYPH / ICON SET STYLE EXPLORATION, ROUND 2
Drafted 2026-08-30. Lives in renders/glyphs/. Round-1 render: `1`.

WHAT ROUND 1 GOT WRONG (checked against docs/ui/ICONS.md)
- Flattened everything to outline. The real vocabulary has a semantic split that
  must survive: a FILLED family (buildings, settlement, HQ, unit, resource pip)
  vs a STROKE-ONLY family (landforms, convoy, market centre, survey, battle,
  under-construction). Filled = "something is installed on this tile";
  stroke-only = "this is the ground / an event", never a thing installed.
- Invented glyphs that do not exist: gas field, energy node, farmland, forest,
  standalone "ore deposit". Resources are ONE diamond pip, recoloured per
  resource. Forest/farmland are terrain cover, not glyphs.
- Corner-bracket frames — drop them. Glyphs draw over live terrain with no
  backing of their own; legibility is the glyph's own job (weight / a dark
  outline on the filled family), never a plate.
- Spec drift: convoy is ONE rightward chevron (two strokes), not three; port is
  a FILLED upward triangle; HQ is a ringed eight-point star (diamond over an
  axis-aligned square, in a ring, dark centre dot).

ROUND 2 SCOPE
Four style directions (G1-G4), each rendering the SAME corrected set below on
one sheet, same grid, so they compare like-for-like. Pick a direction, then it
graduates into ICONS.md and the real src/ui/icons.cpp vector code.

=============================================================================
FIXED CONSTRAINTS (every sheet)
=============================================================================
- Background near-black #0F0F14. Single accent: gold #E8A33D (the settled menu/
  shell accent). No second colour on the sheet — in-game the fill encodes owner
  identity, but the style sheet shows the shapes in one colour.
- Flat vector. No gradients, no glow, no texture, no frames/brackets.
- Every glyph centre-anchored, designed to read inside a 2r box, legible at 16px.
- HONOUR THE FILLED / STROKE SPLIT (see groups below). This is the round-1 miss.
- One sheet, labelled grid, ~1536x1024, grouped by family with family headers.

=============================================================================
THE CORRECTED SET (from ICONS.md)
=============================================================================
FILLED family — solid gold silhouette + a 1px dark inner outline so it holds
over any terrain:
  - Extraction site .... faceted 8-sided ore chunk, wider than tall, corner-cut facets
  - Processing facility . filled square
  - Port ............... filled upward triangle
  - Inland logistics hub  filled flat-top hexagon with a small dark centre dot
  - Military base ...... filled shield, flat top, shoulders tapering to a point
  - Building (none) .... filled circle / dot (also the launchpad fallback)
  - Settlement ......... 1-5 filled towers on a baseline, middle tallest,
                         tapering to the edges (show the 3-tower version)
  - HQ ................ ringed eight-point star: a diamond over an axis-aligned
                         square, enclosed by a ring, dark centre dot
  - Unit .............. humanoid: filled circle head over a filled triangle body
  - Resource pip ...... filled diamond, NO outline (the one filled exception)

STROKE-ONLY family — no dark outline, stroke only:
  - Under construction  crane silhouette: mast, angled boom, back-stay brace, short hook
  - Convoy ........... ONE rightward chevron (two strokes meeting at a right point)
  - Market centre .... circle outline with a centred cross (+), arms ~60% of radius
  - Settlement (razed) two hollow, outline-only tower shells of unequal height on
                       a faint rubble baseline
  - Unknown ......... question mark (hook arc, short stem, dot)
  - Survey badge .... magnifying glass (lens circle + diagonal handle)
  - Activity ....... concentric pulse: a filled core ringed by a signal ring
  - Battle ......... two crossed blades, each a stroked shaft with a short
                     cross-guard bar near its base. NOT a bare X (the X means
                     "dismiss" everywhere else in the chrome)

LANDFORM family — stroke-only, and NO dark outline (a filled landform would read
as "installed"). Only four; plains / highland / valley draw nothing:
  - Mountain ....... twin peaks sharing a saddle, open at the feet, NO baseline
  - Canyon ........ two level rim shoulders split by a narrow incision cutting below them
  - Crater ........ a flattened bowl: wide low ellipse with a raised near-rim arc in its lower half
  - Rift .......... a single jagged fissure running top to bottom (the only zigzag)

CORP EMBLEM shapes — identity, not type; six geometric primitives:
  circle / square / triangle / diamond / hexagon / pentagon (outline, shown as a row)

LENS glyphs — the six on the minimap bar:
  Corp / Country / Resource / Market / Population / Continent (stroke, shown as a row)

=============================================================================
G1 — TWO-WEIGHT, SPEC-FAITHFUL   (the round-1 miss, done right)
=============================================================================
Icon sheet for a near-future corporate 4X, background #0F0F14, single gold
#E8A33D. Render the corrected set with the FILLED family genuinely filled —
solid gold silhouettes each carrying a 1px near-black inner outline — and the
STROKE and LANDFORM families as clean 2px hairline strokes with no fill and no
dark outline. The two weights should be obviously different at a glance: a
building reads as a solid mark, a landform as a line drawing. Flat vector,
geometric, no frames. Grouped labelled grid, family headers, 16px-legible.

=============================================================================
G2 — HEAVIER / STAMPED   (legibility over busy terrain)
=============================================================================
Same corrected set and same filled/stroke split as G1, but everything heavier:
thicker strokes (3px), bolder and slightly simplified filled shapes, tighter
internal negative space — a "stamped into metal" weight. Tests whether the
round-1 thin line is too delicate to read over textured oblique terrain.
Background #0F0F14, single gold #E8A33D, flat, no frames. Labelled grid.

=============================================================================
G3 — GEOMETRIC-MINIMAL / SCHEMATIC   (matches the tech-tree HMI language)
=============================================================================
Same corrected set and filled/stroke split, reduced to the fewest possible
straight lines and hard angles — curves only where a shape genuinely needs one
(the ring on HQ, the crater bowl). Every glyph on an implied 30/60/90 grid,
industrial-schematic / HMI feel, consistent terminal cuts. Background #0F0F14,
single gold #E8A33D, flat, no frames. Labelled grid.

=============================================================================
G4 — ONE FACET OF DETAIL   (character without texture)
=============================================================================
Same corrected set and filled/stroke split as G1, but each filled glyph gains
ONE internal facet line, notch or cut for character — the extraction chunk's
crystal facets, a single chevron seam on the shield, one ridge line on a tower —
still perfectly flat, no shading. Stroke family stays clean. Richer than G1
without adding a second colour or any texture. Background #0F0F14, single gold
#E8A33D, no frames. Labelled grid.
