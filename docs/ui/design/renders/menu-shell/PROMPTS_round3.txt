PROJECT IO — IN-GAME SHELL STYLE EXPLORATION, ROUND 3
Drafted 2026-08-30, after round-2 verdicts. Lives in renders/menu-shell/.

ROUND 2 VERDICTS (Joe)
- S2 "Observatory + weight" is the clear winner. THE BASE for round 3.
  (Hairline shell chrome + solid dark slabs on the Selection band / comms dock /
  minimap frame + bold stencil section labels + filled-gold active nav slot.)
- The thing liked in S3 is its SECOND colour — it breaks the shell up so the
  panels don't all read as one monochrome field. Want to bring a second colour
  onto the S2 base. (The minimap, once designed, will also add variety — but
  test the second colour now.)
- Confirmed: the FILLED-IN nav glyph on selection (S2's filled-gold active slot)
  is liked — keep it.

ROUND 3 SCOPE
S2 base, unchanged, in all three variants. The only new variable is HOW a second
colour is deployed. Second colour = saturated cyan #3FC9E8 (already the house
pair with amber — tech-tree EARNED amber / LOCKED cyan is SETTLED, so this stays
consistent). Each variant assigns the two colours a different JOB.

=============================================================================
SHARED BASE  (S2 — identical in T1, T2, T3)
=============================================================================
In-game screen of a near-future corporate 4X. Background near-black #0F0F14.
Bone white #ECE7DD primary text, dim grey secondary. Type: thin geometric sans,
with BOLD STENCIL for panel section labels.
Chrome: the left icon rail, top data strips and top-right time panel are drawn
as thin 1px hairline outlines with no fills. The Selection band (bottom-centre),
the comms dock (bottom-left) and the minimap frame (bottom-right) are SOLID DARK
SLABS (#141419) with a hard 1px edge. The active nav-rail slot is a FILLED
block (not an outline) — keep this.
Map: central canvas at a LOW OBLIQUE angle ~30-40 degrees above horizontal
(close-up game view, per renders/map/it1). Semi-representational textured
terrain with slight relief and flat shadows, small buildings / settlement
skylines. NO hex grid except the hovered tile (faint bone outline) and the
selected tile (accent outline).
1280x720. Layout ref: ~/io_shell_captures_20260830/nav_rail_default.png
Style refs: ../refs/ON-STYLE/mono, ../refs/ON-STYLE/hexclean.

=============================================================================
T1 — CYAN AS STRUCTURE / NAVIGATION
=============================================================================
Gold #E8A33D = action, selection, your-corp, positive figures, and the filled
active nav slot. Cyan #3FC9E8 = the persistent navigation and structure layer:
inactive nav-rail glyphs and their slot outlines, the minimap frame and its 6
lens glyphs, the slab edges on the comms dock and Selection band, and section
divider rules. Result: the shell scaffold reads cool, the things you act on
read warm. + SHARED BASE.

=============================================================================
T2 — CYAN AS THE OPEN-LEDGER LAYER
=============================================================================
The always-on shell (rail, top strips, comms, minimap) stays exactly S2 —
bone / gold, no cyan. Cyan #3FC9E8 appears ONLY on an OPEN fold-out ledger:
show one ledger open along the left column (e.g. the Corporation overview), and
give its slab edge, its stencil header label and its column rules cyan. While
that ledger is open its nav-rail slot's FILLED block is cyan (not gold), tying
the lit slot to the cyan panel. So an open menu is visibly a different layer
from the persistent shell. + SHARED BASE.

=============================================================================
T3 — CYAN AS THE DATA CHANNEL
=============================================================================
Gold #E8A33D = actions, selection, active nav slot fill. Cyan #3FC9E8 = every
numeric readout and its micro-chart: the "BALANCE / STOCKPILE / NET" figures and
the NET sparkline, the corp roll-up numbers, price ticks, any gauge fill. Body
text stays bone white. Structure stays hairline / bone. Result: the data reads
as its own bright channel against a quiet shell. + SHARED BASE.
